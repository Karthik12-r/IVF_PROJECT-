Fertility Foods Guide
A comprehensive guide to fertility-boosting foods and essential nutrients
Food Categories for IVF Success
Foods that boost egg quality
  • Berries (blueberries, strawberries)
  • Leafy greens (spinach, kale)
  • Omega-3 foods (salmon, walnuts)
Foods that support hormone balance
  • Flax seeds and chia seeds
  • Avocados and healthy fats
  • Whole grains and complex carbs
Foods rich in antioxidants
  • Dark chocolate (in moderation)
  • Green tea and herbal teas
  • Citrus fruits (oranges, lemons)
Foods rich in protein & good fats
  • Paneer and Greek yogurt
  • Eggs and lean proteins
  • Chickpeas and legumes
Essential Nutrient Breakdown

Foods to Limit or Avoid During IVF
  • Caffeine above 200mg/day
  • Processed and packaged foods
  • Excess sugar and artificial sweeteners
  • Alcohol in any form
  • Trans fats and fried foods
  • High mercury fish (tuna, swordfish)
  • Unpasteurized dairy products
  • Raw or undercooked eggs

Safe and effective exercises to support your body during IVF

IVF-Safe Exercises by Phase
During Stimulation Phase
  • 20-30 min walking
  • Gentle yoga (no inversions)
  • Pelvic floor exercises (Kegels)
  • Light swimming
During 2-Week Wait
  • Slow walking (15-20 min)
  • Light stretching
  • Deep breathing exercises
  • Mindfulness meditation
Complete Exercise Routines

  1. March in place for 1 minute
  2. Arm circles (forward and backward) - 30 seconds each
  3. Gentle neck rolls - 1 minute
  4. Shoulder shrugs and rolls - 1 minute
  5. Deep breathing - 1 minute

  2. Cat-Cow Pose - 3 minutes gentle spinal movement
  3. Child's Pose (Balasana) - 3 minutes relaxation
  4. Seated Forward Bend - 2 minutes hamstring stretch
  5. Corpse Pose (Savasana) - 5 minutes final relaxation

Exercises to Avoid During IVF
  • Running or jogging
  • Jumping or high-impact activities
  • HIIT (High-Intensity Interval Training)
  • Heavy weight lifting
  • Twisting abdominal poses
  • Hot yoga or intense heat
  • Contact sports
  • Anything causing strain or fatigue
