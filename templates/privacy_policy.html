Privacy Policy & Data Usage

1. Data Collection & Usage

We collect personal and medical data solely for the purpose of providing IVF tracking, AI predictions, and wellness insights. This includes medical history, cycle data, and uploaded documents.

2. AI & Research Disclaimer

Research Prototype: This application uses Artificial Intelligence (AI) models which are currently in a research prototype phase. Predictions and insights are based on synthetic and historical datasets and should not be considered as absolute medical diagnoses.

Synthetic Data: Some features of this system have been trained using synthetic medical data to ensure privacy and model robustness.

3. Data Security

Your data is encrypted and stored securely. We do not share your personal identifiable information (PII) with third parties without your explicit consent.

4. User Consent

By using this application, you consent to the processing of your data for the purposes described above. You acknowledge that AI predictions are probabilistic and should be verified by a medical professional.

5. Ethics Statement

We adhere to strict ethical guidelines regarding the use of AI in healthcare, prioritizing patient well-being, transparency, and fairness.

Important: This tool is a support aid and does not replace professional medical advice. Always consult your doctor.
