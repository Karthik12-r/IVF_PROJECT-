Relaxation & Meditation Resources
Breathing Exercises

Deep breathing helps activate your body's relaxation response. Try these techniques:

  • Box Breathing: Inhale 4s, hold 4s, exhale 4s, hold 4s
  • 4-7-8 Breathing: Inhale 4s, hold 7s, exhale 8s
  • Diaphragmatic Breathing: Breathe deeply into your belly
Mental Relaxation

Techniques to calm your mind:

  • Progressive Muscle Relaxation
  • Body Scan Meditation
  • Guided Imagery
  • Mindful Awareness
Self-Care During IVF
Physical Self-Care
  • Get adequate rest and sleep
  • Maintain gentle exercise like walking or yoga
  • Eat nutritious, balanced meals
  • Stay hydrated
  • Take your medications as prescribed
Emotional Self-Care
  • Allow yourself to feel all emotions
  • Connect with supportive people
  • Consider counseling or support groups
  • Practice daily relaxation
  • Celebrate small victories
Note: These mindfulness exercises are for relaxation and emotional well-being support. They are not a substitute for professional medical care. Always follow your healthcare provider's advice.
